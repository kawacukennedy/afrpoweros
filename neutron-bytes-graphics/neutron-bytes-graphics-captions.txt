Captions, credit/source text, and placement instructions
for the three graphics accompanying:

"Africa's Nuclear Buildout, by the Numbers"
Neutron Bytes submission — Kennedy Kawacu

====================================================================
Figure 1 — graphic-1-planned-capacity.png
====================================================================
CAPTION
Planned nuclear capacity by African country (GW), with first-grid
target year where announced. Seven countries have announced capacity
plans; South Africa's IRP 2025 (5.2 GW by 2039) and Egypt's El Dabaa
construction program (4 x VVER-1200) are the furthest advanced.

PLACEMENT
Near the "preparing tier" section — after the paragraph introducing
Kenya, Ghana and Rwanda.

====================================================================
Figure 2 — graphic-2-milestone-timeline.png
====================================================================
CAPTION
Key milestones in Africa's civilian nuclear programs, 2015-2026:
from the Russia-Egypt agreement for El Dabaa to first concrete
(2022), South Africa's IRP 2025 and Koeberg licence extension
(2025), and the 2026 advance of Kenya, Rwanda and DR Congo.

PLACEMENT
After the "operating reference point" section, OR between the Egypt
and preparing-tier sections, as a visual bridge.

====================================================================
Figure 3 — graphic-3-status-scoreboard.png
====================================================================
CAPTION
The continental scoreboard: 20 African countries by civilian nuclear
program status — one operating (South Africa), one under construction
(Egypt), seven preparing, ten exploring, and DR Congo with research
applications only.

PLACEMENT
As a lede/opening graphic near the top of the piece to set the scene.
